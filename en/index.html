Free assignment — the key lease provision

Clause 5.1.2 of the lease: the tenant may transfer its rights and obligations to a third party, sublease the plot, pledge the lease rights and contribute them to a company's charter capital — without the landlord's consent, notification is sufficient. Clause 7.1: reorganisation of the parties or a change of the plot's owner is not grounds for termination.

In practice this means an investor can enter through any structure — purchase, assignment, or a joint venture contribution — without applying to the municipality for approval.

02 — what the asset is

Not a plot of land, but a prepared site

A former grain receiving facility 12 kilometres by rail from the Blagoveshchensk dry port. Three things rarely found together: a long-term land title with free assignment, fourteen registered buildings, and a private railway track connected to the station.

The future owner defines what the site becomes. We disclose what is known about it and show which uses it objectively allows.

core of the asset

Railway

700 m · in ownership

A 700-metre railway track, cadastral number 28:14:010311:73, built in 1992. Ownership registered on 24.04.2018, no encumbrances.

The site directly borders the railway right-of-way — no level crossing or passage over third-party land is required. The connection to public tracks is restored under the technical conditions of the infrastructure owner; the application has been submitted.

Land title

11.07 ha · until 2075

110,652 m² of settlement land, permitted use — industrial purposes. Lease agreement No. 22/26-ZUri of 19.06.2026, registered on 23.06.2026. No encumbrances, seizures or claims. Assignment, sublease, pledge and charter-capital contribution are allowed without the landlord's consent.

Buildings

14 units · 12,286.6 m²

Fourteen registered buildings in company ownership: grain warehouses, a four-storey tower, a weighbridge house, a brick substation. The buildings require reconstruction. A court-appointed construction expert examination confirmed that the concrete strength grades meet the applicable standards and that reconstruction of thirteen buildings for their original purpose is feasible.

03 — location

Where it is

Srednebelaya, Ivanovsky municipal district, Amur Region. The site adjoins the right-of-way of the Blagoveshchensk — Belogorsk railway line; the connecting station is Srednebelaya. It sits inside the region's raw-material zone and within an hour's drive of two processing plants.

  • 12 kmto the Blagoveshchensk dry port by rail
  • 60 kmto Belogorsk, agricultural processing
  • ~70 kmto the international Russia — China road crossing: the Blagoveshchensk — Heihe bridge and the Kani-Kurgan customs checkpoint
  • 75 kmto Blagoveshchensk
  • 3–5 kmto the federal highway

The location places the site between the field and the processing plant, and at the same time on the export route: a dry port 12 kilometres away by rail and a border crossing into China about 70 kilometres away. Which flow becomes the primary one is defined by the owner's project.

Distances are given per the rightholder's data and are subject to verification during project design.

04 — possible uses

What the site allows

Directions the site objectively allows, with the constraints of each. The final use is defined by the owner.

Grain and soybean intake terminal

Truck intake from farms, cleaning, storage and rail dispatch to processing. Historically the site's profile. Sanitary gap: 50 m for flat storage, 100 m for an elevator.

Open storage yard

Aggregates, rolled metal, timber, concrete products, empty containers. Deployed in stages, no capital construction required at the first stage.

Inland container yard

Accumulation of empty containers and bagging of agricultural cargo for dispatch through the dry port 12 km away — an established format on the China route.

Wagon storage and marshalling

The 700-metre track holds about 45 wagons. Requires track restoration and inclusion in the infrastructure owner's list of tracks suitable for storage.

Rail-side fertiliser warehouse

Two-season logic: fertilisers arrive in winter and spring, grain ships in autumn. Constraint: above 50 tonnes the sanitary gap is 300 m, which requires separate study within settlement boundaries.

Production base, technology park

About 89 % of the area is free for new construction. The decisive factor is available electrical capacity: a 10 kV line is connected, the available capacity is being confirmed with the grid operator.

05 — title

Legal structure

The asset is structured so that a buyer can enter and exit without approvals from the local authority.

49 years, until June 2075

The lease was concluded on 19 June 2026 and registered in the state register on 23 June 2026. The term covers the full life cycle of an industrial project, including payback.

Assignment without consent

The lease expressly allows transfer of rights to a third party, sublease, pledge and contribution to charter capital without the landlord's consent, subject to notification.

Change of owner does not break the lease

Reorganisation of the parties or a change of the plot's owner is not grounds for unilateral amendment or termination. A share deal does not put the lease at risk.

Title confirmed by court, clean in the register

Ownership of the railway track was registered in 2018 on the basis of a court decision that entered into force. No seizures, mortgages or third-party claims are registered against the land or the buildings.
