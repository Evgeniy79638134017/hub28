Free assignment — the key lease provision

Clause 5.1.2 of the lease: the tenant may transfer its rights and obligations under the agreement to a third party, sublease the plot, pledge the lease rights and contribute them to a company's charter capital — without the landlord's consent, notification is sufficient. Clause 7.1: reorganisation of the parties or a change of the plot's owner is not grounds for termination.

In practice this means an investor can enter through any structure — purchase, assignment, or a contribution to a joint venture — and none of them requires an application to the municipality for consent.

02 — what the asset is

Not a plot of land, but a prepared site

A former grain receiving facility 12 kilometres by rail from the Blagoveshchensk dry port. Three things rarely found together: a long-term land title with free assignment, fourteen registered buildings and a private railway track connected to the station.

The future owner defines what the site becomes. We disclose what is known about it and show which uses it objectively allows.

03 — logistics

Where it is

Srednebelaya, Ivanovsky municipal district, Amur Region. The site adjoins the right-of-way of the Blagoveshchensk — Belogorsk railway line; the connecting station is Srednebelaya. It sits inside the region's raw-material zone and within an hour's drive of two processing plants.

  • 12 kmto the Blagoveshchensk dry port by rail
  • 60 kmto Belogorsk, the region's soybean processing centre
  • ~70 kminternational Russia — China crossing: the Blagoveshchensk — Heihe road bridge and the Kani-Kurgan customs checkpoint
  • 75 kmto Blagoveshchensk
  • 3–5 kmto the Blagoveshchensk — Belogorsk federal highway

The site stands where two flows cross. The domestic one runs between the farms of the region's south-west and processing in Belogorsk, 60 kilometres away. The export one runs 12 kilometres by rail to the Blagoveshchensk dry port and about 70 kilometres by road to the international Russia — China crossing, where the Blagoveshchensk — Heihe bridge and the Kani-Kurgan checkpoint operate. Which flow becomes the primary one is defined by the owner's project.

Distances are given per the rightholder's data and are verified during design. The distance to the dry port is by rail, the others are by road.

04 — why now

The region is turning its raw-material flow inwards

The Amur Region ranks first in Russia for soybean production, about 40 % of the national harvest. The 2025 crop was 1.7 million tonnes, the 2026 plan is about 1.8 million tonnes. Until recently the surplus was shipped out of the region. That is now changing.

Sixty kilometres from the site, in Belogorsk, new processing capacity is coming on stream: from late 2026 domestic soybean processing in the region grows from 450 thousand tonnes to around 1.5 million tonnes a year. The direction of the agricultural flow changes — instead of shipping out of the region, the crop is gathered towards domestic processors. The site sits on that route.

Export infrastructure is developing in parallel. The Blagoveshchensk dry port — 12 kilometres away by rail — opened in May 2026, receives up to two container trains a day and is scaling simultaneous container storage to 15,000 TEU by 2030. The Kani-Kurgan road checkpoint has operated 16 lanes since December 2025, with a capacity of 862 vehicles a day.

Preferential regime

The site lies in the area covered by the "Amurskaya" advanced special economic zone (TOR — the preferential regime for the Russian Far East). Applying the regime to this site requires its inclusion within the zone's boundaries — the procedure exists and the application route is known. The minimum capital investment threshold for a resident is 500,000 ₽, which keeps the regime available even to a small project.

The cadastral quarter of the site is not currently listed within the zone, so the regime does not apply automatically. The timing of the procedure depends on the authorities and we do not forecast it. A detailed analysis of the applicable support measures and resident requirements is provided on request.

06 — title

Legal structure

The asset is put together so that a buyer can enter the project and exit it without approvals from the local authority. Four provisions carry that structure.

Forty-nine years, until June 2075

The lease was concluded on 19 June 2026 for 49 years and registered in EGRN on 23 June 2026. The term covers the full life cycle of any industrial project, including the payback period, and removes the question of renewal from the investor's planning horizon.

Assignment without the landlord's consent

The lease expressly grants the tenant the right, within the term of the agreement, to transfer its rights and obligations to a third party, sublease the plot, pledge the lease rights and contribute them to charter capital — without the landlord's consent, subject to notification. In practice that means four available formats: sale of the lease right, sublease, a bank pledge, and a contribution to the charter capital of a joint company.

A change of owner does not break the lease

The agreement provides that reorganisation of the parties and a change of the plot's owner are not grounds for unilateral amendment or termination. A deal at the level of the legal entity — a change of shareholders, reorganisation, an investor entering the capital — creates no risk for the lease.

Title tested in court and clean in the register

Ownership of the railway track was registered in 2018 on the basis of a court decision that entered into force and the 2012 sale agreement — both documents are named in the EGRN extract as grounds for registration. No seizures, mortgages, judicial claims or expropriation decisions are registered against the plot or the buildings.

Constraints to allow for in the project

The site lies within settlement boundaries and residential development adjoins it from the south-east. The size of the sanitary protection zone depends on the class of the facility: grain storage — 50 metres, an elevator — 100 metres, a mineral fertiliser warehouse above 50 tonnes — 300 metres, a machinery yard with repairs — 300 metres, a customs terminal — 500 metres.

What this means on the ground. From satellite imagery, individual homesteads abut the southern and eastern boundaries, while the northern part of the site is clear: from there to the nearest housing is about 250–300 metres. The building footprint therefore shifts north, and the southern part remains for access roads, parking and planting. Distances are traced from satellite imagery and are subject to measurement on site — that is the first step in working through any scenario.

Two power lines owned by third parties cross the site: 0.4 kV and 10 kV overhead lines. Their protection zones are 2 and 10 metres on each side respectively. Within these corridors, construction, storage and parking of machinery require the grid operator's approval.

Natural conditions: the village lies 4 kilometres from the Belaya river and is not on the list of flood-prone settlements kept by the Amur hydrometeorological centre.

We deliberately list the constraints on the first page. A buyer can verify each of them in a day or two, and it is better for the conversation to start from the full picture than from an unpleasant discovery during a visit.

How a project on this site begins

The order of the new owner's first actions. The timing depends on the authorities and the grid operator, so we do not forecast it — but the sequence is exactly this.

  1. 01 Technical conditions for the connection An application to the infrastructure owner to restore the connection to public tracks. It defines both the cost of restoring the track and the permitted volumes of wagon delivery.
  2. 02 Request for available capacity An application to the grid operator: how much capacity is available on the 10 kV feeder. The answer determines which scenarios are feasible at all — from a warehouse to a data centre.
  3. 03 Measuring the sanitary gaps A field measurement of distances from the future building footprint to the nearest housing. It determines the class of facility that can be placed and the position of the footprint.
  4. 04 Engineering surveys Geology and an assessment of the existing foundations against the loads of the future project — a silo park, a warehouse, a production building.

We have walked this path ahead of you: the application for the technical conditions of the connection has already been submitted, and the facts gathered are handed over to the buyer together with the asset.

08 — deal formats

How you can enter the project

The deal structure is open to discussion, there are no rigid frames. The lease allows disposal of the right without the landlord's consent, so five formats are available — from an outright purchase to a long-term lease.

Investment with a share split

The investor enters the capital, the parties agree the shares and develop the site together. A format for those who want to share both the investment and the result with the current owner.

Joint venture

The lease rights are contributed to the charter capital of a new company and the partner contributes financing. Contribution of the rights to charter capital is expressly allowed by the lease and requires no landlord's consent.

Assignment of the lease with the buildings

The lease right passes to the acquirer for the remaining term — until 2075 — and the buildings are transferred at the same time. The landlord's consent is not required, notification is sufficient.

Acquisition of the company

Purchase of the shares in the company that owns the buildings and the lease right. The asset transfers as a whole and the lease is not renegotiated when the shareholders change.

Long-term lease of the site

The site, in whole or in part, is subleased for an operational task — without changing the tenant and without a deal on the asset. The landlord's consent is not required.

10 — questions

Frequently asked questions

What condition are the buildings in?

The buildings require reconstruction. A court-appointed construction expert examination confirmed that the concrete strength grades meet the applicable standards and that reconstruction for the original purpose is feasible.

Is the railway track operational?

The track is in ownership and registered in the state property register. The connection to public tracks is restored under the technical conditions of the infrastructure owner; the application has been submitted.

Can the land be bought out?

Russian law grants the owner of buildings on a plot an exclusive right to purchase that plot, with no public auction. The procedure for determining the price is set by the municipality.

Can the lease be resold or pledged?

Yes. The agreement expressly allows transfer of the rights to a third party, sublease, pledge and contribution to charter capital without the landlord's consent.

Are there any encumbrances or disputes?

No encumbrances, seizures or third-party claims are registered. The court proceedings of previous years are closed.

How much electrical capacity is available?

A 10 kV line is connected and a substation building stands on the site. The available capacity is being confirmed with the grid operator.

What is the price?

Terms are discussed individually depending on the chosen deal structure.

Can a foreign investor take part?

Yes. Participation goes through a Russian legal entity — acquiring a stake in the company that holds the asset, setting up a joint venture, or taking an assignment of the lease to a Russian company. The lease is held by a Russian company, and the plot is not in a border area where foreign ownership of land is restricted. The specific structure is worked out together with your lawyers.

Housing is close by — will sanitary zones rule out every scenario?

They rule out specific activities — chemicals and production with a large sanitary zone — not everything. Logistics, storage, rolling-stock parking and light processing all pass. Individual homesteads abut the southern and eastern boundaries, while the northern part of the site is clear — about 250–300 metres to the nearest housing. A buyer will not meet a surprise at the design stage.

A state industrial park is being created nearby — why this site?

State parks solve a different task and work by different rules: restrictions on activities, competitive entry and dependence on budget decisions. This site is private and controllable: build it, lease it out or sell it at the owner's decision. These are different products, not competitors for the same buyer.

Has the dry port already taken the border logistics?

A growing terminal creates demand around itself for back-up capacity: a buffer, storage, accumulation of empty containers. Sitting twelve kilometres from a developing port works in the site's favour.

The area is overgrown — do we clear it at our own cost?

Clearing means vegetation and debris, with no demolition of buildings: they are registered and stay in place. The scope of work is understood and is taken into account when the deal terms are discussed. Part of the work can be done before the deal if the parties agree.

No water and no gas — how critical is that?

It is a budget line of the project, as on any unprepared site. Power is there: a 10 kV line is connected and a transformer substation building stands on the site; the available capacity is being confirmed with the grid operator. Water is solved with a borehole.
